02 // Who we are

A startup built by three complementary minds

GVR Caliber was founded by three partners who kept meeting the same problem from different angles: teams drowning in manual work, data they could not act on, and software that never quite fit. We started the company to fix that end to end.

One of us lives in enterprise automation and process design. One of us builds machine learning and agentic AI systems. One of us ships production web and mobile products. Together we cover the full path from a rough idea to a running system your team relies on.

Our mission is simple: put intelligent automation within reach of teams that were told it was too complex, too expensive, or too far off.

  • AutomationProcess design and RPA
  • IntelligenceML and agentic AI
  • ProductWeb and mobile delivery

03 // Capabilities

Six modules. One delivery team.

Select a module to unlock the detail.

Rule-based work, handled by software robots that never tire.

We map high-volume, repetitive processes and rebuild them as reliable bots in UiPath, Power Automate, or Automation Anywhere. You get audit trails, exception handling, and dashboards, not a black box.

  • Process discovery and ROI modelling
  • Attended and unattended bot development
  • Orchestration, monitoring, and support

Automation that reads, decides, and adapts, not just clicks.

We combine RPA with document AI, natural language understanding, and decision models so processes handle unstructured inputs and edge cases on their own. Fewer handoffs, fewer queues.

  • Intelligent document processing
  • Process mining and orchestration
  • Human-in-the-loop review workflows

Goal-driven AI agents that plan, use tools, and get work done.

We design multi-step agents that reason over your systems, call APIs and tools, and complete tasks with guardrails and observability. Built for real workflows, not demos.

  • Agent architecture and tool integration
  • Retrieval, memory, and evaluation pipelines
  • Safety rails, logging, and cost controls

Turn raw data into forecasts, segments, and decisions.

From data pipelines to deployed models, we build the analytics layer your team acts on: demand forecasting, churn and risk scoring, anomaly detection, and clear dashboards.

  • Data engineering and feature pipelines
  • Predictive and classical ML models
  • Dashboards and decision tooling

Fast, modern web apps built on React and a solid backend.

We ship production web applications: internal tools, customer portals, dashboards, and SaaS products, with clean architecture, testing, and CI so they stay maintainable.

  • React front ends and design systems
  • APIs, auth, and cloud deployment
  • Performance, accessibility, and monitoring

Cross-platform mobile apps with React Native and Expo.

One codebase, both platforms. We build and ship iOS and Android apps with React Native and Expo, including offline support, push, and over-the-air updates.

  • React Native and Expo development
  • App Store and Play Store release
  • Analytics, crash reporting, and OTA updates
